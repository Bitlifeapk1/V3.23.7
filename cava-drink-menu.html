Quick Answer: Pricing

A standard fountain drink at the self-serve station runs $3.60–$3.85 depending on your market. Bottled options — Spindrift sparkling water and Open Water — run $3.00–$3.25. Kids drinks, including organic milk, chocolate milk, and apple juice, are a flat $3.00 at most locations.

Pricing shifts slightly by region: expect the higher end (~$3.85) in dense urban markets like Manhattan, and the lower end (~$3.60) in cities like Austin.

💡 Dining in gets you free refills on every fountain drink — juices, teas, and sodas — for the length of your visit, and you're free to mix flavors as many times as you like.

The "Secret Menu" Combos

Because the fountain station is fully self-serve, regulars have quietly built their own combinations. Blending two house juices — or a juice with tea — controls both sweetness and flavor.

🍹

The Tropical Cutter

Lemonade + Pineapple Apple Mint

Half classic lemonade, topped off with pineapple apple mint. The lemon's tartness keeps the pineapple from tipping into overly sweet.

🌺

The Floral Palmer

A lower-sugar spin on an Arnold Palmer. Unsweetened iced tea cuts straight through the floral sweetness of the blueberry lavender juice.

Health & Dietary Facts

Zero-Sugar & Keto

Juices and lemonade carry 20g+ of sugar. Safe options: unsweetened iced tea and Spindrift (zero/near-zero sugar).

Caffeine Content

House juices and lemonade are caffeine-free. Iced tea and cola-style sodas contain caffeine.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does CAVA offer free refills on drinks?
Yes — dining in gets unlimited refills on house juices, iced tea, and sodas for the duration of the visit, since the fountain station is self-serve.
Are CAVA juices made with real fruit?
Yes — the juices use real fruit purée, pressed vegetable juice, and fresh herbs rather than flavored syrup concentrate. Pulp settling is normal.
Can you order just a drink without buying food?
Yes — they can be purchased on their own at the register. However, free refills are generally reserved for guests dining in with a food order.
Are CAVA's drinks vegan and dairy-free?
Every drink except the kids' organic milk and chocolate milk is vegan and dairy-free.
