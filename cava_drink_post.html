Choosing the wrong beverage can add well over 300 unexpected calories, or clash with the bold, savory spices already in the meal. Unlike fast-casual chains that lean on bag-in-box syrups and artificial dyes, CAVA runs a scratch-made beverage program built on cold-pressed juices, steeped teas, and Fair Trade cane sugar.

That scratch-made approach is what separates CAVA's drink station from a typical soda fountain: the juices are batched fresh, the teas are brewed rather than powdered, and the sweeteners are traceable. Whether you're grabbing a quick lunch near the University of Maryland or taking a dinner break around Dupont Circle in Washington D.C., the right beverage can neutralize fiery harissa and cut through rich garlic dressings — or quietly wreck an otherwise balanced meal if you're not paying attention.

This guide breaks down the full CAVA drink menu for 2026: price ranges, calories by cup size, sugar content, and which combinations actually work with the food.

Quick Answer: How Much Are Drinks at CAVA?

A standard fountain drink at the self-serve station runs $3.60–$3.85 depending on your market. Bottled options — Spindrift sparkling water and Open Water — run $3.00–$3.25. Kids drinks, including organic milk, chocolate milk, and apple juice, are a flat $3.00 at most locations.

Pricing shifts slightly by region: expect the higher end (~$3.85) in dense urban markets like Manhattan, and the lower end (~$3.60) in cities like Austin.

Dining in gets you free refills on every fountain drink — juices, teas, and sodas — for the length of your visit, and you're free to mix flavors as many times as you like.

CAVA House-Made Juices & Lemonade (Prices & Macros)

CAVA batches its juices fresh in-house, mixing filtered water, real fruit purée, and cane sugar by hand each morning rather than shipping in concentrate. Because there's no stabilizer keeping things uniform, it's normal to see fruit pulp or herb flecks settle at the bottom of the dispenser over the course of a shift.

The figures below are approximate estimates for a standard 16 oz pour, no ice — filling a full 22 oz cup will run meaningfully higher, and a 12 oz kids' cup will run lower. For an exact figure on the day you order, check CAVA's nutrition calculator in the app.

🍍

Pineapple Apple Mint

💰 $3.60–$3.85 🔥 ~180–220 cal 🍯 ~20–25g sugar

For diners who want something juice-like without the calorie hit of the lemonade, this is the lighter alternative — tropical pineapple sweetness balanced by crisp apple cider and a mint finish. It pairs cleanly with simpler bowls, like chicken and rice, without competing with the flavors.

🫐

Blueberry Lavender

💰 $3.60–$3.85 🔥 ~220–260 cal 🍯 ~30–35g sugar

Floral and earthy rather than straightforwardly sweet, this one splits opinion on its own. Most regulars don't drink it straight — they cut it at the fountain with classic lemonade, which tames the lavender and adds a tart edge that balances the whole cup.

🥒

Cucumber Mint Lime

💰 $3.60–$3.85 🔥 ~250–300 cal 🍯 ~30–38g sugar

If the goal is putting out a fire from the Spicy Lamb Meatballs or a heavy hand with skhug, this is the drink for it. Pressed cucumber, fresh mint, and a tart lime finish make it one of the more effective palate resets on the menu — sweet enough to be refreshing, but not cloying.

🍋

Classic Lemonade

💰 $3.60–$3.85 ⚠️ ~450–480 cal 🍯 ~50g sugar

Worth flagging directly: this is the highest-sugar drink on the entire menu, and it's not close — it runs around 50g of sugar in a standard serving. Real lemon juice and cane sugar give it a genuinely sharp, traditional tartness, but if sugar intake is a concern, this is the one to skip or cut with unsweetened iced tea.

Note: CAVA occasionally rotates in limited-time or seasonal juice flavors. Check the menu board or app at your location for anything beyond this core lineup before assuming it's a permanent item.

Fresh Brewed Iced Teas (Zero-Calorie Options)

For anyone avoiding sugar entirely, CAVA brews iced tea fresh daily — no powdered mix. Given that a fully loaded bowl can already run 500–900+ calories, pairing it with a zero-calorie drink is the simplest lever for keeping a meal from tipping too high.

🧊

Unsweetened Iced Tea

💰 $3.60–$3.85 ✨ 0 cal ✨ 0g sugar

Zero-calorie and zero-sugar, this is the best base for the half-and-half combinations covered further down — the bitterness cuts the sugar load of any juice it's mixed with.

🥤

Sweetened Iced Tea (Sweet Tea)

💰 $3.60–$3.85 🔥 ~120–150 cal 🍯 Moderate sugar

The sweetened counterpart, brewed the same way but finished with added sugar. A straightforward classic if you want tea without the citrus or floral notes of the juices. Meaningfully lower than the lemonade, but not sugar-free.

Fountain Sodas & Bottled Drinks

Not everyone wants juice or tea — CAVA's carbonated lineup covers that without falling back on standard big-brand soda syrup at most locations.

Maine Root Fair Trade Sodas

At many locations, CAVA's fountain sodas come from Maine Root, a Fair Trade brand that sweetens with real cane sugar rather than high-fructose corn syrup. Availability and exact flavor lineup can vary by store, so confirm what's on tap at your location. Priced $3.60–$3.85.

Spindrift Sparkling Water

At $3.00–$3.25 a can, Spindrift is the closest thing on the menu to a genuinely "clean" carbonated option: it's flavored with real squeezed fruit juice rather than concentrate, and runs just a few calories with zero added sugar. Lemon and Half-Tea with Lemon are commonly stocked flavors, though this varies by store.

Kids Beverages (Organic Milk, Chocolate Milk & Apple Juice)

The Kids Meal lineup keeps things simple: organic apple juice (no added sugar), organic lowfat milk (a solid source of calcium and protein), and organic chocolate milk. All are a flat $3.00.

The "Secret Menu": Best Half-and-Half Drink Combos

Because the fountain station is fully self-serve, regulars have quietly built their own combinations over time. Blending two house juices — or a juice with tea — is the easiest way to control both sweetness and flavor without asking anyone behind the counter.

🍹

The Tropical Cutter

🍋 Lemonade + 🍍 Pineapple Apple Mint

Half classic lemonade, topped off with pineapple apple mint. The lemon's tartness keeps the pineapple from tipping into overly sweet, and the combination holds up well against garlic-heavy chicken bowls.

🌺

The Floral Palmer

🧊 Unsweetened Iced Tea + 🫐 Blueberry Lavender

A lower-sugar spin on an Arnold Palmer: unsweetened iced tea cuts straight through the floral sweetness of the blueberry lavender juice. Moderate caffeine, without the sugar crash a straight juice would bring.

The Clean Refresh

🍋 Lemonade + 🥒 Cucumber Mint Lime

Diluting the lemonade with cucumber mint lime keeps the herbal, sweet-tart profile while cutting the sugar load roughly in half. Some regulars take it further by adding a splash of Spindrift for a DIY sparkling version.

Dietary Guide: Healthiest CAVA Drinks

Liquid calories are easy to lose track of — they don't register the same way solid food does, which makes them an easy place for a "healthy" meal to quietly go over budget.

Best Zero-Sugar & Keto-Friendly Options

For strict keto or diabetes management, the juices and lemonade are off the table — even the lighter ones carry 20g+ of sugar per serving. The safe options are unsweetened iced tea and Spindrift sparkling water, both of which come in at zero or near-zero sugar.

Which CAVA Drinks Are Caffeine-Free?

The house-made juices and the classic lemonade are naturally caffeine-free. Iced tea (sweetened or unsweetened) contains caffeine, as does cola-style soda where available; other soda flavors like lemon-lime and root beer are typically caffeine-free.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Does CAVA offer free refills on drinks?

Yes — dining in gets unlimited refills on house juices, iced tea, and sodas for the duration of the visit, since the fountain station is self-serve.

What is the lowest-calorie drink at CAVA?

Excluding plain water, unsweetened iced tea is the zero-calorie option. For a carbonated pick, Spindrift Lemon comes in at just a few calories per can.

Are CAVA juices made with real fruit?

Yes — the juices use real fruit purée, pressed vegetable juice, and fresh herbs rather than flavored syrup concentrate, which is why pulp and fiber settling in the cup is normal rather than a quality issue.

Can you order just a drink at CAVA without buying food?

Yes — the fountain station and bottled drinks can be purchased on their own at the register, without ordering a bowl or pita. Free refills, however, are generally reserved for guests dining in with a food order, so a drink-only purchase may not come with the same refill policy.

Are CAVA's drinks vegan and dairy-free?

Every drink except the kids' organic milk and chocolate milk is vegan and dairy-free. The house-made juices, lemonade, iced tea, sodas, and Spindrift sparkling water all use plant-based or water-based bases with no dairy, honey, or animal-derived sweeteners.

Prices, calories, and available flavors are approximate and can vary by location and season. Confirm exact figures at your local CAVA or through the CAVA app's nutrition calculator before ordering, especially if you're managing a specific dietary restriction.
