CAVA Drink Menu Prices & Calories

Most diners spend ten minutes customizing the perfect Mediterranean bowl, only to completely overlook their drink choice at the fountain station. Here is the ultimate guide to getting it right.

Choosing the wrong beverage can add well over 300 unexpected calories, or clash with the bold, savory spices already in the meal. Unlike fast-casual chains that lean on bag-in-box syrups and artificial dyes, CAVA runs a scratch-made beverage program built on cold-pressed juices, steeped teas, and Fair Trade cane sugar.

That scratch-made approach is what separates CAVA's drink station from a typical soda fountain: the juices are batched fresh, the teas are brewed rather than powdered, and the sweeteners are traceable. Whether you're grabbing a quick lunch near the University of Maryland or taking a dinner break around Dupont Circle in Washington D.C., the right beverage can neutralize fiery harissa and cut through rich garlic dressings — or quietly wreck an otherwise balanced meal if you're not paying attention to the sugar content.

Quick Answer: Pricing

A standard fountain drink at the self-serve station runs $3.60–$3.85 depending on your market. Bottled options — Spindrift sparkling water and Open Water — run $3.00–$3.25. Kids drinks, including organic milk, chocolate milk, and apple juice, are a flat $3.00 at most locations.

💡 Pro Tip: Dining in gets you free refills on every fountain drink — juices, teas, and sodas — for the length of your visit, and you're free to mix flavors as many times as you like.

The "Secret Menu" Combos

Because the fountain station is fully self-serve, regulars have quietly built their own combinations. Blending two house juices — or a juice with tea — controls both sweetness and flavor.

The Tropical Cutter

Lemonade + Pineapple Apple Mint

Half classic lemonade, topped off with pineapple apple mint. The lemon's tartness keeps the pineapple from tipping into overly sweet.

The Floral Palmer

Unsweet Tea + Blueberry Lavender

A lower-sugar spin on an Arnold Palmer. Unsweetened iced tea cuts straight through the floral sweetness of the blueberry lavender juice.

The Clean Refresh

Lemonade + Cucumber Mint Lime

Diluting the lemonade keeps the herbal, sweet-tart profile while cutting the sugar load roughly in half. Very refreshing.

Dietary & Health Guide

🟢 Zero-Sugar & Keto

Juices and lemonade carry 20g+ of sugar. Safe options: unsweetened iced tea and Spindrift (zero/near-zero sugar).

⚡ Caffeine Content

House juices and lemonade are caffeine-free. Iced tea and cola-style sodas contain caffeine.

🥤 Bottled Options

Spindrift Sparkling Water ($3.00–$3.25) is flavored with real squeezed fruit juice with zero added sugar.

🧃 Kids Beverages ($3.00)

Organic apple juice (no added sugar), organic lowfat milk, and organic chocolate milk.
