Overview & Origins

What Is CAVA? A Fast-Casual Mediterranean Restaurant

Understanding the fast-casual brand redefining healthy customizable dining across the United States.

CAVA is a fast-casual restaurant built around Mediterranean food. Guests build their own bowl or pita by picking a base, a protein, a few dips, unlimited toppings, and a dressing, all assembled at the counter. The chain started as one sit-down restaurant in Bethesda, Maryland, in 2011, and grew into a national brand from there.

As of April 2026, CAVA Group runs 459 restaurants across 29 states and Washington, D.C., a figure confirmed in the company's own SEC filing. CAVA is also a public company, trading on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker CAVA since 2023.

The CAVA Mezze Concept Explained

CAVA mezze refers to the small-plate, shared-dip tradition found across Greek, Lebanese, and broader Mediterranean cooking. CAVA built its menu around this idea by offering six scratch-made dips, such as hummus, tzatziki, and harissa, free with every bowl or pita. Most fast-casual chains treat dips as a paid extra. CAVA treats them as the core of the meal, which is a large part of why regulars often say the dips, not the protein, are the real reason they keep coming back.

How CAVA Compares to Typical Fast Food

CAVA is often called the Mediterranean answer to fast food, and the comparison holds up in a few specific ways. Fast food menus generally lean on fried items and heavy sauces built for shelf life. CAVA's proteins are grilled or roasted in-house, its dips are made fresh daily, and its bases include whole grains and greens. It is still a for-profit chain built around margins like any other restaurant, but the ingredient sourcing sits closer to a sit-down Mediterranean spot than a typical drive-through.

Nutritional Deep Dive

Is CAVA Food Actually Healthy?

An honest, unbranded look at CAVA's nutritional macro distribution, cooking oils, and calorie traps.

Most CAVA bowls fall between 480 and 860 calories with 30 to 50 grams of protein and a high share of vegetables and whole grains, which puts CAVA comfortably ahead of most fast-casual competitors on paper.

⚠️ Cooking Oil Transparency

CAVA cooks with a blend of sunflower oil and olive oil across most of the menu, despite marketing that leans heavily on its olive oil and Mediterranean positioning.

Calories can also climb past 1,000 quickly if you add multiple dips, extra cheese, avocado, and a rich dressing on top of a grain-heavy base. Whether CAVA is healthy for you personally depends far more on how you build your specific bowl than on the restaurant's overall branding.

How to Keep a CAVA Bowl Under 600 Calories:

  • Pick a greens-only base: SuperGreens or arugula instead of rice saves 150–280 calories.
  • Choose lean grilled chicken: Delivers 34g protein for only 200 calories.
  • Stick to two dips: Combine one creamy dip (tzatziki at 40 cal) with one flavorful dip (hummus or roasted eggplant at 45 cal).
  • Ask for dressing on the side: Using half a portion of Greek vinaigrette saves close to 70–100 calories.

Fast-Casual Face-Off

CAVA vs Other Fast-Casual Chains

How CAVA stacks up against Chipotle and Sweetgreen on pricing, included dips, and value.

CAVA vs Chipotle

Mezze vs Mexican

CAVA is often called the Mediterranean version of Chipotle because both use an assembly-line build-your-own model.

  • Free Dips: CAVA includes 6 scratch-made dips free. Chipotle charges $2.50+ extra for Guacamole or Queso.
  • Price: CAVA ($12–$16) runs slightly higher than Chipotle ($10–$13), but the free dips close the gap.
  • Nutrition: CAVA offers whole grains (RightRice, Black Lentils) and fresh greens with higher protein diversity.

CAVA vs Sweetgreen

Mediterranean vs Salad Lab

Sweetgreen focuses on warm grain bowls and seasonal produce with similar plant-based offerings.

  • Dip & Dressing Policy: Sweetgreen includes dressing only; CAVA includes both 3 dips and dressings free.
  • Pricing: Sweetgreen bowls run $13–$17, roughly in line with CAVA, but CAVA offers heavier portions per dollar.
  • Flavor Density: CAVA's whipped feta and spicy harissa deliver a bolder Mediterranean profile.

Final Verdict on the CAVA Menu

The CAVA menu works because the ordering system is simple even though the ingredients behind it are not. Whether you are managing a dietary restriction, training for athletic performance, or deciding between the Harissa Avocado Bowl and a build-your-own steak pita, the core value of CAVA comes down to complete control over what actually ends up in your meal.
