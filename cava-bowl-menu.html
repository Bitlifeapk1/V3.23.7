How Much Does a CAVA Bowl Cost?

A CAVA bowl costs between $10.97 and $19.89, depending on how you build it. Build-your-own orders sit at the lower end of that range, while signature bowls with premium proteins like steak, lamb, or salmon push closer to the top. Prices also shift by location, and big cities or airports usually charge 10 to 15 percent more than the national average.

💡 Pro Tip: Nutrition Accuracy

CAVA bowl calories usually fall between 400 and 900. Published nutrition numbers for the same item can vary between sources, sometimes by 50 to 80 calories. The most accurate numbers always come straight from CAVA's own app, which calculates calories based on your exact custom build.

Build Your Own Bowl: Step-by-Step

  • 1

    Choose Your Base

    Your base sets the calorie floor. Light options like SuperGreens run 30-50 calories, while heartier choices like saffron rice run 200-290 calories.

  • 2

    Choose Your Protein

    Grilled chicken offers the best protein-to-calorie ratio on the menu (~33g protein for 250 cal).

  • 3

    Choose Dips & Spreads

    You get up to three free dips. Hummus and tzatziki form the base layer in most signature bowls. Crazy Feta® adds a spicy kick.

  • 4

    Add Toppings

    Toppings are free and unlimited. Pickled onions add almost no calories while cutting through richer proteins.
