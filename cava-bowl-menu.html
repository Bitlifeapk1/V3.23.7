Pricing & Nutrition

How Much Does a CAVA Bowl Cost?

Understanding the true cost and macros of your custom bowl.

A CAVA bowl costs between $10.97 and $19.89, depending on how you build it. Build-your-own orders sit at the lower end of that range, while signature bowls with premium proteins like steak, lamb, or salmon push closer to the top. Prices also shift by location, and big cities or airports usually charge 10 to 15 percent more than the national average.

CAVA Bowl Calories: Full Nutrition Guide

CAVA bowl calories usually fall between 400 and 900, and the number depends mostly on your base, protein, and how many dips or toppings you add. A light bowl made with greens, grilled chicken, and one dip can stay under 500 calories. A fully loaded signature bowl with rice, a second protein, and multiple dips can pass 850.

💡

Pro Tip: Accuracy

Published nutrition numbers for the same item can vary between sources, sometimes by 50 to 80 calories. The most accurate numbers come straight from CAVA's own app, which calculates calories based on your exact build.

Best CAVA Bowl for Every Goal

📉

Lowest Calories

A romaine or SuperGreens base with grilled chicken, tzatziki, roasted eggplant dip, and yogurt dill dressing lands around 400 to 450 calories.

💪

Maximum Protein

Ordering a double protein, most commonly two servings of grilled chicken, pushes total protein toward 60 to 66 grams. Add black lentils for an extra 13-18g.

🥑

Keto / Low-Carb

Skip rice and grains entirely. Use romaine and arugula as your base, choose grilled chicken or braised lamb, add Crazy Feta®, tzatziki, and avocado.

Build Your Own Bowl: Step-by-Step

A build your own bowl at CAVA follows five steps.

  • 1
    Choose Your Base: Your base sets the calorie floor. Light options like SuperGreens run 30-50 calories, while heartier choices like saffron rice run 200-290 calories.
  • 2
    Choose Your Protein: Grilled chicken offers the best protein-to-calorie ratio on the menu (~33g protein for 250 cal).
  • 3
    Choose Dips & Spreads: You get up to three free dips. Hummus and tzatziki form the base layer in most signature bowls. Crazy Feta® adds a spicy kick.
  • 4
    Add Toppings: Toppings are free and unlimited. Pickled onions add almost no calories while cutting through richer proteins.
  • 5
    Choose Dressing: One dressing comes free. Lemon herb tahini is generally considered the most popular dressing on the menu.
