How Much Does a CAVA Bowl Cost?

A CAVA bowl costs between $10.97 and $19.89, depending on how you build it. Build-your-own orders sit at the lower end of that range, while signature bowls with premium proteins like steak, lamb, or salmon push closer to the top. Prices also shift by location, and big cities or airports usually charge 10 to 15 percent more than the national average.

CAVA Bowl Calories: Nutrition Guide

CAVA bowl calories usually fall between 400 and 900, and the number depends mostly on your base, protein, and how many dips or toppings you add. A light bowl made with greens, grilled chicken, and one dip can stay under 500 calories. A fully loaded signature bowl with rice, a second protein, and multiple dips can pass 850.

Here's something worth knowing

Published nutrition numbers for the same item can vary between sources, sometimes by 50 to 80 calories for something as small as falafel. The most accurate numbers come straight from CAVA's own app, which calculates calories based on your exact build.

Build Your Own Bowl: Step-by-Step

A build your own bowl at CAVA follows five specific steps at the counter.

1

Choose Your Base

Your base sets the calorie floor. Light options like SuperGreens run 30-50 calories, while heartier choices like saffron rice run 200-290 calories.

2

Choose Your Protein

Grilled chicken offers the best protein-to-calorie ratio on the menu (~33g protein for 250 cal).

3

Choose Dips & Spreads

You get up to three free dips. Hummus and tzatziki form the base layer in most signature bowls. Crazy Feta® adds a spicy kick.

4

Add Toppings

Toppings are free and unlimited. Pickled onions add almost no calories while cutting through richer proteins.

5

Choose Dressing

One dressing comes free. Lemon herb tahini is generally considered the most popular dressing on the menu.
