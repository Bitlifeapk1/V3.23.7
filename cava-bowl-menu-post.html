How Much Does a CAVA Bowl Cost? (2026 Price Guide)

A CAVA bowl costs between $10.97 and $19.89, depending on how you build it. Build-your-own orders sit at the lower end of that range, while signature bowls with premium proteins like steak, lamb, or salmon push closer to the top. Prices also shift by location, and big cities or airports usually charge 10 to 15 percent more than the national average. Knowing this range ahead of time stops you from assuming every bowl costs the same.

CAVA Bowl Calories: Full Nutrition Guide

CAVA bowl calories usually fall between 400 and 900, and the number depends mostly on your base, protein, and how many dips or toppings you add. A light bowl made with greens, grilled chicken, and one dip can stay under 500 calories. A fully loaded signature bowl with rice, a second protein, and multiple dips can pass 850.

💡 Here's something worth knowing: published nutrition numbers for the same item can vary between sources, sometimes by 50 to 80 calories for something as small as falafel. This happens because recipes change over time. The most accurate numbers come straight from CAVA's own app, which calculates calories based on your exact build.

CAVA Build Your Own Bowl: Step-by-Step Guide

A build your own bowl at CAVA follows five steps, and knowing what each one controls makes the process faster at the counter.

  • 🟢 Step 1: Choose Your Base - Your base sets the calorie floor and texture of the whole bowl. Light options run 30 to 50 calories, while heartier choices like saffron rice run 200 to 290 calories.
  • 🟢 Step 2: Choose Your Protein - Your protein choice affects both calories and texture. Grilled chicken offers the best protein-to-calorie ratio on the menu.
  • 🟢 Step 3: Choose Your Dips & Spreads - You get up to three free dips with every bowl. Hummus and tzatziki form the base layer in most signature bowls.
  • 🟢 Step 4: Choose Your Toppings - All toppings are free and unlimited. Pickled onions add almost no calories while cutting through richer proteins.
  • 🟢 Step 5: Choose Your Dressing - One dressing comes free. Lemon herb tahini is generally considered the most popular dressing on the menu.

Best CAVA Bowl for Every Goal

📉 Lowest Calories

A romaine or SuperGreens base with grilled chicken, tzatziki, roasted eggplant dip, and yogurt dill dressing lands around 400 to 450 calories.

💪 Maximum Protein

Ordering a double protein, most commonly two servings of grilled chicken, pushes total protein toward 60 to 66 grams. Add black lentils for an extra 13-18g.

🥑 Keto / Low-Carb

Skip rice and grains entirely for this one. Use romaine and arugula as your base, choose grilled chicken or braised lamb, add Crazy Feta®, tzatziki, and avocado.

Frequently Asked Questions About CAVA Bowls

What is the most popular CAVA bowl?
The Chicken + Rice Bowl is widely regarded as the top seller, thanks to its balanced, low-risk flavor. The Harissa Avocado Bowl is frequently cited by reviewers and repeat customers as a favorite.
What is the healthiest CAVA bowl?
The SuperGreens + Grilled Chicken combination, at around 480 calories with 33 grams of protein, offers the strongest balance of low calories and high protein among common builds.
How much does a CAVA bowl cost?
Prices range from $10.97 for a basic build-your-own order up to $19.89 for premium signature bowls. Location and protein choice are the two biggest factors in where your order lands.

Final Thoughts: Which CAVA Bowl Should You Order?

If you're new to CAVA, start with the Chicken + Rice Bowl. It shows you what the menu does well without asking you to make many decisions. Once you know the base flavors, try the Harissa Avocado Bowl and Spicy Lamb + Avocado Bowl next, and check out the newer sweet potato and salmon bowls if you haven't visited recently. Building your own bowl with a lean protein, two well-chosen dips, and a lighter dressing remains the most reliable way to control the outcome, no matter which combination you land on.
